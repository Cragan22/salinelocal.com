How Saline Local Works

The best businesses in Saline County aren't always the ones with the biggest ad budget. Saline Local is where you go to find the local gems, the businesses your neighbors already trust even if you haven't heard of them yet.

Our listings are free, but invitation-only. That means you're not seeing who paid the most to be here. You're seeing business owners who earned their spot because they're proud of what they do and stand behind their work.

Knowing the people behind a business matters. When you work with a neighbor who cares about their name in this town, you get a different level of accountability than you'd get from some outfit three states away.

We like having a little fun with it too, and we've got some fun ways to help you discover local favorites in the works. Stay tuned.

From the founder

Hi, I'm Chris Ragan.

I built Saline Local because I got tired of watching my neighbors drive past great local businesses to spend money at a chain — not because they preferred it, but because they didn't know what was right down the road.

I personally interview every business owner before they're featured here. I ask the questions you'd ask a trusted friend: Is it worth my time? Do they actually stand behind their work? If the answer is yes, they earn a spot on Saline Local.

This isn't a platform built by an algorithm in San Francisco. It's a neighbor-to-neighbor project, built in Saline County, for Saline County.

— Chris Ragan, Saline County resident
