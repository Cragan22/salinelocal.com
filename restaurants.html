Best Restaurants in Saline County

From family-run taquerias to neighborhood pizza joints, these are the sit-down spots and quick bites where Saline County actually eats — no drive-thru corporate menus, just real food from people who live here.

6 restaurants serving Benton, Bryant, and surrounding areas.

Blue House Bakery & Cafe Verified & Trusted

Bryant, AR

Blue House Bakery & Cafe started as two sisters' ten-year dream of opening a small-batch bakery — a dream that became real in 2019, named for the childhood home their dad built alongside their oldest brother.

Chili's Grill & Bar

Benton, AR

Chili's Grill & Bar is a family-friendly stop in Benton serving classic Tex-Mex and American fare — burgers, fajitas, and bottomless chips — in a Southwestern-style setting that's easy to bring the whole crew to.

Don Pepe's Gourmet Burritos & Tacos

Bryant, AR

I still remember the first time I stopped in for a carne asada burrito — I've been going back every couple weeks since. Don Pepe's isn't chasing a trend or copying a chain's menu; it's a family recipe served the same honest way it's always been served, right here in Bryant.

Hideaway Pizza

Benton, AR

At Hideaway Pizza, you'll find the perfect ingredients for a great time — a unique, vibrant spot for family, friends, a date night, or just yourself, with real pizza that stays unapologetically loyal to its tried-and-true recipes.

Italy in Town

Benton, AR

Italy in Town is a locally owned restaurant serving made-from-scratch Italian food in a cozy date-night atmosphere — authentic cuisine, fine wines, and weekly specials designed to be fresh and a little elevated.

Red Robin

Benton, AR

Since 1969, Red Robin has been the place that does burgers better than anyone — Bottomless Steak Fries, gourmet burgers, and shareable bites that turn into shareable moments, right off I-30 in Benton.
